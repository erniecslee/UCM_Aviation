RTG OPTIMUM searches TO/TO-1/TO-2 for the lowest legal %N1 — TO-1/TO-2 have no published FCOM field/climb chart, so their runway-specific limit weight is estimated by scaling TO(26K)'s real chart by thrust ratio, and their flex temp uses that rating's published ceiling rather than a weight-specific solve. INTX/I-C are display-only — not wired into the calculation (no taxiway-intersection or Improved Climb data in the dataset).
